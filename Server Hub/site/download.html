What you need

Bedrock Hub works part by part, so you only need what the parts you use need.

Setting it up

First run

  1. Put Bedrock-Hub-3.1.5.exe anywhere, such as your desktop, and open it.
  2. On the Mod Library page, press Add mods to bring in your add-ons.
  3. On the Bridge page, sign the bridge in with the code it shows.
  4. On the Server page, type your public IP address.
  5. On the Worlds page, load a world, add its mods and press Host.
  6. Press Start, then run Status and setup test to check everything.

Where your files live

  • %USERPROFILE%\HMBC-Library: your mod library
  • %USERPROFILE%\HMBC-Bridge: the Microsoft sign-in and bridge settings
  • %USERPROFILE%\HMBC-Server: the server, its settings and worlds
  • %APPDATA%\Bedrock Hub: app settings and logs

Updating Bedrock Hub is just replacing the .exe. These folders stay as they are.
