Vanilla End Expansion (server edition)

Adds Purpur Wastes and End Desert areas to the outer End islands, Endermite Nests, Purpur Ice, Purpur Gel and the Purpur Gel Block.
The central End island is left untouched.

Original add-on by its author (not named in the original files); modified for private server use.
Requires Minecraft 1.26.10 or newer. Install both the behaviour pack and the resource pack.
Commands: /scriptevent vaen:status
